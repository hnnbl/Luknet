- Foreground: `#f0f0ff` (near-white with slight blue/violet tint)
- Primary accent: `#7c3aed` (violet-600 — the hero purple)
- Primary accent hover: `#8b5cf6` (violet-500 — neon shift on hover)
- Neon highlight: `#a78bfa` (violet-400 — glow-level brightness)
- Secondary surface: `#0d0d1a` (very dark navy, for card backgrounds)
- Card border: `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.2)` (subtle purple border on cards)
- Muted text: `#6b7280` (gray-500 — secondary text)
